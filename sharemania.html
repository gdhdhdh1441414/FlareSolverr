    Tate McRae - SO CLOSE TO WHAT??? (deluxe) [48kHz · 24bit]

    Format : PCM - AIFF
    Format settings : Big
    Bit rate mode : Constant
    Bit rate : 2 304 kb/s
    Channel(s) : 2 channels
    Sampling rate : 48.0 kHz
    Bit depth : 24 bits

    HDTracks Released 2025-11-21

    New Tracks:

    “TRYING ON SHOES”
    The grand orchestral opening of “TRYING ON SHOES” is the perfect start to the deluxe edition. While “So Close To What” gave us a hint of McRae’s breathier vocal register, it is employed at full force for the first time here. McRae takes a slow and dreamy approach to describe the process of trying to move on from a past partner. “Trying on shoes, putting on glitter / Anything to make me a little less bitter,” she sings. Fans have also speculated that the partner in question is none other than LAROI himself. Lines like “You roll your eyes at all my shows and God knows I see it” allude to events that correspond to their public lives.
    “ANYTHING BUT LOVE”
    “ANYTHING BUT LOVE” is a clear standout and personal favorite from the new tracks. McRae is unabashedly vicious as she sing-raps “My fans hate you, my friends hate you, and Ryan hates you, and I do too,” making her bitter feelings towards the song’s subject very clear. However, beyond its harsh lyrics, “ANYTHING BUT LOVE” employs an almost R&B-infused sound that the singer has rarely explored in her past work. The rap-like verses are reminiscent of her single “exes,” but the smooth-layered vocals heard on the chorus, string-infused backtrack and trap-inspired beat is new territory for McRae. The style suits her surprisingly well, and hopefully she continues to experiment with it in the future.
    “NOBODY’S GIRL”
    On “NOBODY’S GIRL,” McRae embraces the split from her relationship. “I am nobody’s girl / Does it mess you up?” She asks tauntingly as she embraces her newfound independence. This track still leans into the classic dance-pop sound of the album’s standard edition, with sirens and electronic production making their way into the chorus. However, McRae still explores new territory as the production breaks away during the bridge of the song, and she delivers a spoken interlude.
    “HORSESHOE”
    “HORSESHOE” is the most introspective and emotional song of the bunch, as McRae gives us a glimpse of the darker side of her rapid ascent to fame. “Got 20,000 people just smiling / And it only takes one to get me spiraling” she writes, indicating that her life is not always as perfect as it appears to the media. McRae grapples with feeling grateful for achieving her dreams while coping with the life changes it spurs. “So, who am I to cry tonight? Such a lucky girl, I know / But where should all my sadness go?”
    “TIT FOR TAT”
    McRae surprise-dropped “TIT FOR TAT” back in September after writing and recording the song in two weeks in Nashville while on tour. The song’s release came after news of her split from LAROI first emerged, and LAROI called her out on his own track. While she never publicly addressed the situation, her track spoke for itself. “TIT FOR TAT” is McRae’s most honest and venomous track to date. “Let’s go song for song, let’s go back to back / Fix your f—ing self, kiss my a– for that,” she sings over a pulsating beat complete with almost-shouting vocals in the chorus. The spite in this track feels authentic and fresh as McRae repeatedly takes stabs at LAROI’s behavior. “I was nothing but respectful, and you know that / Why you changing up the narrative to write?” She asks in response to his breakup song. McRae came to set the record straight, and it seemed to work, as “TIT FOR TAT” became one of her highest charting tracks to date, debuting at #3 on the Billboard Hot 100.

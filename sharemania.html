    Michael Jackson - Thriller (1982) [Original First Pressing, No Dynamic Range Compression]

    Evidently, the initial pressing failed to include the co-producer credit for Michael Jackson. In terms of sound, it feels absolutely amazing. It still surpasses the majority of recordings made today, whether digital or analog.

    Nevertheless, the sales soared, and the productions needed to increase. Epic promptly addressed this credit, and the majority of the Thriller LPs and CDs feature the revision that lists MJ as the co-producer.

    Once sales surged, the quality of pressing declined while attempting to match the demand.

    Smile was scheduled for release in mid-to-late October 1997, with the radio edit and remixes of Is It Scary as its B-side – likely timed to coincide with the Halloween season. The planned release date can be inferred from its catalogue number, which places it among other Epic singles issued in October, as well as from Michael’s scheduled performances of Smile on November 6 and 8, 1997, in Europe. These performances were intended to serve as the official music video to promote the single. However, after Michael decided not to perform, Sony cancelled the release just days before it was set to hit stores and ordered all copies destroyed. Only a handful are known to have survived. Track 04 was originally not part of this release.

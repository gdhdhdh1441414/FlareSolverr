    Pink Floyd - The Dark Side Of The Moon [50 Anniversary Edition] (2023) Blu-Ray Audio



    Pink Floyd's The Dark Side of the Moon: 50th Anniversary on Blu-ray. Remastered by James Guthrie. The Blu-ray Features the Dolby Atmos Mix, 5.1 Surround Sound Mix and the Remastered Stereo Mixes.
    One of the most iconic and influential albums ever, Pink Floyd’s The Dark Side Of The Moon continues to celebrate its 50th Anniversary. First released as part of The Dark Side Of The Moon 50th Anniversary Box Set, this is the first time that the new remaster of the classic album will be available on its own.
    The 50th anniversary edition Blu-ray features the Dolby Atmos mix, 5.1 surround sound mix, as well as remastered stereo mixes. Packaged in a die cut digipak this Blu-ray also includes a postcard, stickers, and 24-page booklet.

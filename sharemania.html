    This contains the uncensored version of the episode "Mum", from the third season of The Shield (2002). It was left censored on ALL streaming services (both the HD and 4K remastered versions) and on the BluRay home media release of the episodes, despite other episodes being left uncensored, due to the scene where David Aceveda gets assaulted by Byz Lats gang members.

    Despite this, it was SOMEHOW left uncensored on the Tubi streaming service when it was added back in November 2025. This is in 720p, in a slightly higher quality than what was seen on the original US DVD release from both 2005 and 2008.

    The rest of the series (both in HD and 4K) can be easily found on other sites, so I'm just archiving this one episode as it's harder to find it in good quality.

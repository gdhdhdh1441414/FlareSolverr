    Traditional animation gets compressed a lot thanks to the AV1 codec. AV1 CRF 19 is supposed to be much better than x264 and also better than x265 at the same respective CRF levels. Important note though. AV1 as a codec requires significantly more video processing power compared to H264. Ideally, you want to have a discrete GPU that supports hardware acceleration for AV1. If your desktop/laptop has no discrete GPU, I would say the bare minimum you need is a 4-core CPU with 8 threads and moderate clock speed. Even if you have an older GPU which does not support hardware acceleration for AV1, your machine will struggle to play AV1 if the video is 2160p and has very high bitrate (probably 20 Mbps and higher).
